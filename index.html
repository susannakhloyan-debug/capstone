HOW IT WORKS

Four steps, one SLA

No sales calls, no open-ended scoping. An application starts a five-day clock, and the clock doesn't reset.

01

Apply

A short, structured application — the business problem, not a sales pitch. Reviewed by the team, not a queue.

02

Scope 5-day SLA

The team turns the application into a concrete, priced proposal within five business days — no exceptions.

03

Deliver

A four-person team — Business Analyst, PM, Designer, and Tech Feasibility Lead — executes against the scoped proposal.

04

Pay on completion

Payment is due once delivery is complete, not before. The risk sits with Bridgeline until the work is done.

THE TEAM

Four roles, one proposal

Every scoped proposal passes through the same four seats — not a rotating cast of subcontractors.

01 · Business Analyst

Turns the ask into a spec

  • Discovery that surfaces what's known, assumed, and still missing
  • Current-state vs. desired-state gap analysis
  • User stories and acceptance criteria the proposal gets priced against

02 · Project Manager

Owns the five-day clock

  • Keeps scoping on schedule against the SLA — every application, every time
  • Coordinates the other three seats so nothing waits on a meeting
  • Single point of contact for the applicant, start to delivery

03 · Designer

Makes the proposal buildable

  • Wireframe and flow specs ready for handoff
  • Makes the scope tangible before a developer ever sees it
  • Screen-by-screen states, not just a happy path

04 · Tech Feasibility Lead

Reality-checks the proposal

  • Flags what's actually buildable in scope and budget
  • Surfaces integration risk before it's priced, not after
  • Keeps the five-day proposal honest, not just fast
